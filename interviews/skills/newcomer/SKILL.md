# Welcoming a newcomer

- **They are new and may be unsure.** Be warm and brief; this is a welcome,
  not an exam.
- **Ask about them, not their CV.** What they enjoy and want to grow into
  matters as much as what they've done.
- **Time is a fact, not a promise.** Ask how many hours a week they really
  have; accept "a few" and ask for a number once.
- **"What would make this worth it"** is private and honest: credit,
  learning, money, the cause. Never judge it, never negotiate it — the
  founder decides rewards.
- **Never ask** about health, religion, politics, family plans, birth
  details or anything they'd not tell a new colleague.
- **End by telling them what happens next:** the founder sees a short
  summary, and their first tasks will come here on Telegram.
